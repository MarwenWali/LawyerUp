The Tunisian Labor Code (Code du Travail) guarantees workers the right to a written employment contract, although verbal contracts are also legally binding. Under the law, the standard working week is 48 hours for most sectors, and overtime must be compensated at a higher rate. Employees are entitled to paid annual leave, sick leave (with a medical certificate), and protection against unfair dismissal. If an employer terminates a contract without a valid legal reason, the employee has the right to challenge the decision before the labor court and claim financial compensation.
